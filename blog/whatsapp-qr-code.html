How to Create a WhatsApp QR Code for Free (Step-by-Step Guide)

WhatsApp has emerged as one of the most well-known messaging applications in the world. Whether you are an entrepreneur, a freelancer, or simply wanting to provide an easier way for people to contact you, there are times when it can be inconvenient for another person to have to save the number associated with your WhatsApp account before sending you a message. The added step means additional work on behalf of the person trying to reach you.

To eliminate this additional work, a WhatsApp QR code allows anybody who scans it to immediately initiate communication with you on WhatsApp without needing to either search for you or type in your mobile number.

In addition, there is no cost associated with creating a WhatsApp QR code, and the entire process takes less than five minutes to complete. This post will provide step-by-step instructions on how to create a WhatsApp QR code at no cost as well as tips for effectively using the code you've created.

What Is a WhatsApp QR Code?

A QR code for WhatsApp is a particular QR code that includes a direct link to your WhatsApp chat. As soon as an individual scans that QR Code with their Smartphone Camera or QR scanner, WhatsApp will open up on their phone so they can send you a message immediately.

The WhatsApp QR Code function would be of great benefit to the following:

  • Small Companies and Business Owners
  • E-commerce Website Owners and Sellers
  • Independent Contractors and Freelancers
  • Customer Service and Support Groups
  • Event Coordinators/Promoters
  • Social Media Creators
  • Personal Networks

Users will no longer have to enter your telephone number before they contact you. They simply have to scan your QR code, and they are connected with you immediately through WhatsApp!

What You Need Before Creating a WhatsApp QR Code

Before getting started you'll need to do the following:

  • Have a WhatsApp account.
  • Have a telephone number (including the country code).
  • Have internet access.
  • Have a free QR code generator.

Optionally create a custom welcome message. After a person scans your code it will appear automatically. This will help you simplify how you communicate with each other.

Example:

“Hi, I am interested to learn about your products.”

Step 1: Create Your WhatsApp Link

Generating a WhatsApp click-to-chat link is your starting point.

The proper way is:

https://wa.me/YourPhoneNumber

In which you must substitute “YourPhoneNumber” with your full phone n

Step 2: Add a Pre-Filled Message (Optional)

If you want people to begin the conversation with a ready-made message, you can add a pre-filled text to your WhatsApp link. This is particularly useful for businesses because customers don't have to think about what to type.

Example:

https://wa.me/919876543210?text=Hello%20I%20would%20like%20to%20know%20more%20about%20your%20services

After scanning the QR code, WhatsApp will automatically open with the message already entered.

Step 3: Choose a Free QR Code Generator

Copy your WhatsApp link, open a trusted QR code generator, choose the URL option, paste the link and generate your QR code.

  1. Open a QR code generator.
  2. Select URL.
  3. Paste your WhatsApp link.
  4. Click Generate QR Code.
  5. Preview the result.

Step 4: Customise Your QR Code

Keep the design simple. Add your brand colours or logo if required, but avoid making the QR code difficult to scan.

  • Brand colours
  • Logo
  • Simple frame
  • Readable pattern

Step 5: Download Your QR Code

Download the QR code as PNG for digital use or SVG for high-quality printing.

Step 6: Test Your QR Code Before Sharing

Scan the QR code using different phones and confirm that the correct WhatsApp chat opens and the pre-filled message appears correctly.

  • Correct phone number
  • Fast scanning
  • Working message
  • No errors
